# Research Protocol — Evidence Before Hypotheses

## The Rule
Collect evidence BEFORE forming hypotheses. Correct sequence: Screen → Blind evidence sweep → Theory formation → Analysis.

## Time Budget (HARD STOPS)

```
Domain 1: Primary Financials       2 hours MAX then stop
Domain 2: Competitive Landscape    2 hours MAX
Domain 3: Demand Chain             1 hour MAX
Domain 4: Expert Landscape         1 hour MAX
Domain 5: Regime and Macro         30 min MAX
─────────────────────────────────────────────
TOTAL: 6.5 hours before hypothesis formation
```

**Hard stop after 6.5 hours.** Additional research ONLY after ACH matrix identifies specific evidence gaps via EVPPI. Unlimited research before hypothesis formation leads to unconsciously building the evidence base that supports the conclusion you're already forming.

## Evidence Sweep (same for every stock)

### Domain 1: Primary Financials (2 hours MAX)
Sources: SEC EDGAR 10-K, 10-Q (last 4 quarters)
Collect: Revenue, margins, FCF, guidance language VERBATIM
Rule: Extract numbers only. No interpretation.

### Domain 2: Competitive Landscape (2 hours MAX)
Sources: Competitor 10-Ks, SemiAnalysis/sector specialist, industry reports
Collect: Market share data, competitor moves, switching cost evidence
Rule: Include evidence that cuts BOTH directions

### Domain 3: Demand Chain (1 hour MAX)
Sources: Customer earnings calls, import/export data, job postings, leading indicators
Collect: Every upstream signal above the company's revenue line
Rule: Minimum 3 layers above revenue

### Domain 4: Expert Landscape (1 hour MAX)
Sources: Analyst reports (summaries only — avoid anchoring), prediction markets, academic papers
Collect: Consensus view AND bear cases
Rule: Collect both sides explicitly before reading either deeply

### Domain 5: Regime and Macro (30 min MAX)
Sources: FRED, FOMC, sector ETF flows, VIX
Collect: Current regime state, historical analogues for this regime
Rule: Collect facts, not predictions

## Output
Raw evidence file — numbers, quotes, data points with sources and dates.
NO analytical language. No "strong," "suggests," "indicates." Just facts.

## Variant Perception Statement (REQUIRED before evidence chain)

After evidence sweep, before running any analysis, write:

```
VARIANT PERCEPTION — [TICKER]

The market believes: [specific consensus view, from Domain 4 expert landscape]
I believe: [what I think is different, from the evidence sweep]
The gap exists because: [why the market is wrong — specific mechanism]
The gap closes when: [what event resolves the disagreement — with date]
```

If you can't write this statement: you don't have a trade yet. You have research. Go back to the evidence sweep and identify what the market might be wrong about. If you can't find anything, the stock is probably fairly valued and should be in WATCHING not ENTERING.

## Three-Source Rule
For any factual claim entering the analysis:
1. Primary source (company filing, official data)
2. Bull interpreter (analyst who sees this as positive)
3. Bear interpreter (analyst who sees this as negative)

If you can't find a bear interpreter: RED FLAG.

## Expert Source Tiers
- TIER 1: Specialist publications (SemiAnalysis, Fabricated Knowledge, Stratechery)
- TIER 2: Academic (SSRN, NBER, CRS)
- TIER 3: Prediction markets (Polymarket, Metaculus)
- TIER 4: Earnings transcripts (Motley Fool, Seeking Alpha — free same-day)

## Contrarian Source Requirement
Every evidence item with IC > 1.0 bits requires an identified contrarian source. If none found, document WHY (not just "couldn't find one").

## Sector Onboarding Gate (BLOCKING — required before first gauntlet in a new sector)

Before analyzing the FIRST stock in any new sector:

```
SECTOR ONBOARDING CHECKLIST

[ ] Identified tier-1 specialist publication for this sector
[ ] Built historical analogue set (n≥15) for sector-specific failure modes
[ ] Mapped demand chain template for this sector's primary revenue driver
[ ] Identified sector-specific leading indicators (minimum 3)
[ ] Found the sector's equivalent of "TSMC monthly revenue" (leading supplier indicator)
[ ] Located prediction market coverage for sector events
[ ] Identified Gabriel's specific domain edge in this sector (if none: don't analyze)

Estimated time: 4-6 hours
CANNOT be skipped or done in parallel with first analysis
```

If the checklist isn't complete, the gauntlet WILL NOT RUN for that sector. This prevents the pattern where demand chain was "to build later" — it's built BEFORE the first analysis.
