# Fundamental Analysis Lens

**Core Question:** Do the numbers support this theory?

## What to Gather (via WebSearch)

Search for: "[TICKER] revenue earnings quarterly results" and "[TICKER] PE ratio market cap financials"

Pull:
- **Revenue**: last 4 quarters, YoY growth rate, QoQ trend
- **Earnings**: EPS actual vs consensus, beat/miss streak
- **Margins**: gross margin, operating margin, net margin — trending up or down?
- **P/E ratio**: current vs 3-year average vs sector average
- **Market cap** and enterprise value
- **Balance sheet**: cash position, debt levels, free cash flow
- **Forward guidance**: what management is saying about next quarter/year

## How to Analyze

Connect EVERY data point back to the theory:
- If theory says "monopoly position" → are margins expanding? (monopolies have pricing power)
- If theory says "growth story" → is revenue actually accelerating or decelerating?
- If theory says "undervalued" → where is P/E relative to historical? What's the PEG ratio?
- If theory says "infrastructure play" → is capex from customers increasing?

Don't just list numbers. Interpret them through the lens of the theory.

## Red Flags to Watch
- Revenue growing but margins shrinking (growth at any cost)
- Earnings beats driven by cost-cutting, not revenue growth
- P/E significantly above historical average with no fundamental justification
- Declining free cash flow despite revenue growth
- Management consistently missing their own guidance

## Output Format

2-4 paragraphs analyzing the financials specifically in relation to the theory.

Then:
```
**Verdict:** SUPPORTS/NEUTRAL/UNDERMINES — [one sentence explaining why]
```

Example: "**Verdict:** SUPPORTS — Revenue growing 80% YoY with expanding margins confirms the infrastructure buildout thesis. P/E at 35 vs 3yr avg of 60 suggests the market is underpricing growth."
