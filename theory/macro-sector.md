# Macro/Sector Analysis Lens

**Core Question:** Does the macro environment help or hurt this theory?

## What to Gather (via WebSearch)

Search for: "[SECTOR] outlook 2026" and "interest rates impact [SECTOR]" and "[TICKER] geopolitical risk"

Pull:
- **Interest rates**: current Fed/BoE rate, direction (hiking/cutting/holding), impact on growth stocks
- **Sector performance**: how is the sector ETF performing? (XLK for tech, etc.)
- **Sector rotation**: is money flowing into or out of this sector?
- **GDP/economic indicators**: recession risk, consumer confidence
- **Geopolitical**: any country-specific risks (China bans, sanctions, tariffs)?
- **Regulatory**: any pending legislation that affects this company/sector?
- **Currency**: USD/GBP movements that affect returns (Gabriel invests in GBP)
- **AI-specific** (if relevant): AI spending trends, enterprise adoption rates, government AI policy

## How to Analyze

The macro environment is the water the stock swims in. Connect it to the theory:
- If theory relies on "massive AI infrastructure buildout" → are hyperscalers actually increasing capex? Any signs of pullback?
- If theory says "growth stock" → rising rates hurt growth stock valuations (higher discount rate). Where are rates headed?
- If theory depends on a specific market → are there geopolitical risks to that market?
- If the theory is US-focused → how does USD/GBP movement affect Gabriel's returns?

Macro is where most retail investors get blindsided. They analyze the company perfectly but miss the tsunami coming from interest rates or geopolitics.

## Red Flags to Watch
- Aggressive rate hikes (kills growth stock valuations)
- Sector ETF in downtrend while individual stock is flat (sector rotation out)
- Geopolitical escalation affecting supply chains
- Regulatory crackdown in the company's primary market
- "AI bubble" narrative gaining mainstream traction (sentiment shift)

## Output Format

2-4 paragraphs analyzing the macro environment relative to the theory.

Then:
```
**Verdict:** SUPPORTS/NEUTRAL/UNDERMINES — [one sentence]
```

Example: "**Verdict:** SUPPORTS — Fed cutting rates benefits growth stocks, hyperscaler capex at all-time highs confirms AI infrastructure thesis. Geopolitical risk (China chip export controls) is real but NVDA has already adapted with compliant chips."
