# Technical Analysis Lens

**Core Question:** Is the chart aligned with the theory's timeline?

## What to Gather (via WebSearch)

Search for: "[TICKER] technical analysis RSI MACD" and "[TICKER] stock chart support resistance"

Pull:
- **RSI**: current value (overbought >70, oversold <30, neutral 30-70)
- **MACD**: signal line position, histogram direction
- **Moving averages**: 50-day, 200-day, golden/death cross status
- **Support/resistance**: key price levels
- **Volume**: recent vs average, any unusual spikes
- **52-week range**: where is price relative to high/low
- **Trend**: higher highs/higher lows (uptrend) or lower highs/lower lows (downtrend)

## How to Analyze

Connect chart action to the theory's timeline:
- If theory is long-term (1-3 years) → short-term RSI overbought doesn't kill the thesis, but a death cross might delay your timeline
- If theory says "entry point now" → is price near support or resistance?
- If theory says "undervalued" → is the chart showing accumulation (rising volume on up days)?
- If theory says "momentum play" → is MACD confirming or diverging?

Technical analysis for this system is NOT about predicting the future. It's about timing — is NOW a good time to enter given the theory?

## Red Flags to Watch
- RSI above 75 (overbought — you might be late to the party)
- Death cross (50-day crossing below 200-day)
- Declining volume on price increases (weak conviction in the move)
- Price at all-time high with RSI divergence (price up but RSI making lower highs)
- Multiple failed attempts to break a resistance level

## Output Format

2-4 paragraphs analyzing the technical picture in relation to the theory and timing.

Then:
```
**Verdict:** SUPPORTS/NEUTRAL/UNDERMINES — [one sentence]
```

Example: "**Verdict:** NEUTRAL — RSI at 55 and price consolidating near 200-day MA. No strong directional signal, but not a bad entry. Chart isn't screaming buy OR sell."
