# Macro Triggers

**Core Question:** What external environment changes would hurt this theory?

## What to Gather (via WebSearch)

Search for: "interest rates outlook 2026" and "[SECTOR] sector risk" and "recession probability" and any geopolitical risk specific to the company

Pull:
- Current interest rate (Fed funds, BoE)
- Rate direction (hiking/cutting/holding)
- Sector ETF performance (XLK for tech)
- GDP growth / recession indicators
- Any company-specific geopolitical risks (e.g., China exposure for NVDA)
- Currency rates (USD/GBP for Gabriel's portfolio)

## Trigger Categories

### Interest Rate Triggers
- **Rate hike beyond threshold**: if Fed funds rate exceeds X%
  - Growth stocks are rate-sensitive (higher rates = higher discount rate = lower valuations)
  - Severity: MINOR (single hike), MAJOR (multiple unexpected hikes)

### Sector Triggers
- **Sector selloff**: if sector ETF drops X% in a month
  - Even good companies get dragged down in sector rotation
  - Severity: MINOR (sector dip, thesis intact), MAJOR (sustained sector outflow)

### Recession Triggers
- **Economic contraction**: if GDP goes negative for 2+ quarters
  - Different companies react differently — some are recession-resistant
  - Severity depends on how theory-dependent the company is on economic growth

### Geopolitical Triggers
- **Country-specific risk**: [specific event] affecting [specific market]
  - E.g., "China bans import of NVDA chips" or "US imposes new AI export controls"
  - Must be specific to how it affects THIS company and THIS theory
  - Severity: varies — trade restrictions on a company's biggest market = MAJOR/EXIT

### Currency Triggers
- **GBP/USD movement**: if exchange rate moves more than X%
  - Gabriel invests in USD stocks with GBP income
  - A strengthening GBP means his USD holdings are worth less in GBP terms
  - Severity: MINOR (not a thesis issue, but affects real returns)

### AI-Specific (if relevant)
- **AI spending pullback**: if hyperscaler capex guidance drops
  - For AI-thesis stocks, this is potentially the most important macro trigger
  - Severity: MAJOR (guidance drop), EXIT (actual capex cuts)

## Output Format

For each proposed trigger:
```
| # | Trigger | Current Value | Threshold | Severity | Theory-Specific? |
```

Include 2-4 macro triggers. Focus on the ones most relevant to THIS theory, not generic macro worry.
